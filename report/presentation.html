🛡️ SAFETY & ERROR CORRECTION
COST-THEORETIC AUTONOMOUS ROLLBACK

The system protects the patient
and corrects itself.

Risk-governed 4-tier policy combined with Bayesian ErrP error potential monitoring.

CONFIDENCE TIERS

Policy Engine

  • EXECUTE ($c \ge 0.75$): Immediate actuation + voice announcement
  • CONFIRM ($0.50 \le c < 0.75$): Single-token prompt to verify intent
  • REJECT ($c < 0.50$): Silent drop, avoids unwanted actuation
  • CAREGIVER: Thermal setpoints <18°C require nurse sign-off
ERRP ROLLBACK

Error Potentials

Monitors frontocentral electrodes ($\mathrm{FCz}$/$\mathrm{Cz}$) for 650 ms post-action.

  • Shrinkage LDA on 72 windowed features
  • Platt scaling for calibrated posterior $q$
  • Saerens prior adjustment for baseline shift
DECISION THRESHOLD

Bayesian Cost $t^*$

t* = 0.55

Derived from asymmetric false-positive vs. false-negative cognitive costs:

$t^* = \frac{C_{\mathrm{FP}}}{C_{\mathrm{FP}} + C_{\mathrm{FN}} - C_{\mathrm{TP}}} = \frac{5}{5+7-2.92} = 0.5507$

ATOMIC RESTORATION: Reverts appliance state & penalizes rejected entity in Knowledge Graph.
